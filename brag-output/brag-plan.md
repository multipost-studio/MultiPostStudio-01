# Brag Plan: MultiPost Studio

## What is this app?
MultiPost Studio is an AI-powered social media operating system — plan, create, publish, engage, and analyze content across nine platforms (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky) from one workspace.

## The angle
Not "a scheduling tool." The whole social workflow — that normally lives in nine different tabs, an AI writing app, a spreadsheet, and a shared inbox — collapsed into one screen. The video proves it by actually walking the loop: type an idea → AI drafts it for every platform → it lands in the queue → the health score goes up → the companion mascot celebrates. Real screens, real copy, no stock footage energy.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Gradient headline slams in over the violet/pink ambient wash: "Your whole social workflow." — the word "workflow" rendered in the product's own serif-italic treatment, exactly as it appears live on the marketing hero.

## Key moments (the middle)
- AI Studio drafting a caption live — text types in, then platform-variant chips (IG / LinkedIn / TikTok / X…) tick across one at a time as the copy re-flows per platform.
- The queue/calendar: a post card drops into a day slot and the 9 platform badges light up around it one by one — the "one queue, every network" claim, shown not told.
- The 0–100 health score counting up on the analytics view while a mini area chart draws in underneath it — "answers, not just analytics" landing as a number, not a paragraph.

## Outro / punchline
The mascot companion (the actual in-app character, glasses + curls) does its "wave" bounce next to the wordmark as the tagline settles: "Plan, create, publish, engage, analyze. One workspace." — then "Get started free — no card needed."

## User flow worth showing
Dashboard greeting → open composer, AI drafts + scores a post → post lands in the calendar/queue across platforms → health score ticks up on analytics. Entry → key action → result, using the product's own dashboard, composer, calendar, and analytics screens rather than marketing mockups.

## Tone
- Preset: `default`
- Creative direction: vibrant, colorful product-in-motion reel — the product's own iridescent violet/pink/sky palette and playful mascot carry the energy; no fake-corporate voiceover posture.
- Interpretation: comfortable pacing, room for each moment to breathe (per the 5-6 scenes / default tone table), motion stays snappy but never rushes a read. Crossfades and clean wipes between scenes, not hard cuts.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 30s (explicit user request — overrides the default 15-25s law)

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#faf8f5` (light) — warm cream, not white
- Accent: `#ec5fa3` (pink/magenta) alongside primary `#6d5ae6` (violet) and info `#3e8fd9` (sky)
- Text: `#14121f` (near-black ink) / muted `#5c5670`
- Display font: Bricolage Grotesque (headings)
- Body font: Work Sans
- Emphasis/serif treatment: Fraunces italic — used live for the word "workflow" in the real hero headline
- Strongest visual element: the gradient-text hero headline + soft violet/pink ambient orb wash + the 2×2 feature bento with tinted card backgrounds (`--block-rose/mint/amber/blue`) and live mini-charts (heatmap / bars / donut / area)

## Share copy (draft)
Plan, create, publish, engage, and analyze — nine platforms, one workspace, and an AI sidekick that actually does the busywork. Meet MultiPost Studio.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, upbeat bed with motion-matched accents — polished, not corporate
- Music: bright, optimistic electronic/pop bed, mid-tempo (~110-120bpm), matching the violet/pink energy — not epic-trailer, not lo-fi
- Music treatment: starts under the hook at moderate volume, brief swell under the publish/queue scene (the busiest visual beat), gentle fade under the outro so the tagline reads clean
- Music cue guidance: no bundled preset selected yet — detect cues at composition time (`npx hyperframes beats`). Target strong cues at: hook slam (0:00), queue platform-badges sequential reveal (~0:15-0:19), health-score count-up start (~0:22). Beat-grid window for the platform-badge sequence: badges land on alternating beats, not every beat, so each is readable.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — the ambient orb/glow behind the hook headline may breathe gently with the music's low end; no waveform bars, no aggressive strobing
- SFX posture: moderate — one soft "type" tick under the AI caption draft, a light card-pop per platform badge landing, a soft chime on the health-score count-up finishing, a gentle "boop" (matching the product's real mascot boop sound) on the outro wave
- Audio-coupled moments: typed caption text in Scene 3, sequential platform-badge reveal in Scene 4, count-up chime in Scene 5, mascot boop in Scene 6
- Restraint rule: never let SFX stack — one sound event per beat max; music never drowns a line of on-screen text; no sound during the hook's first 0.3s (let the type land silently, then the bed fades in)

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3s
Cream background, soft violet/pink ambient orb drifting behind center. Eyebrow pill ("AI-powered social operating system") fades in first (0.3s), then the headline slams in: "Your whole social workflow" with "workflow" in Fraunces italic. Hold for full read.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: quiet open, headline lands on a soft musical downbeat
Audio-coupled idea: none — headline is a slam-in, not a typed reveal
Music: bed fades in under the headline landing, low volume
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Dashboard reveal — 4.5s
Cut to the real dashboard screen: "Good afternoon" greeting header, then the priority cards ("posts scheduled," "pending approvals," "conversations") and performance stat tiles animate in in sequence, left to right.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 priority cards arrive one by one, ~0.4s apart, then stat tiles below fade in as a group
Audio intent: light, confident build
Audio-coupled idea: soft card-pop per priority card arrival
Music: bed continues, gentle rhythmic pulse under the card pops
Transition mood: soft wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — AI Studio composing — 5.5s
Composer screen. A short caption types in character by character (real product voice, e.g. "New drop is live — link in bio 🔗" minus emoji per brand copy, keep it plain). Once typed, AI Studio scores the post (a small score chip ticks up), then platform-variant chips tick across one at a time: Instagram → LinkedIn → TikTok → X, each briefly highlighting as the copy visibly reflows for that platform's voice/length.
Sequential/interaction: yes — caption types out, then 4 platform chips cycle one at a time (~0.6s each)
Audio intent: playful, inventive — this is the "smart" beat
Audio-coupled idea: subtle key-tick sounds under the typing; soft tick per platform-chip cycle
Music: brief upward motif under the platform-chip cycle
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Publish / one queue, every network — 6s
Calendar/queue view. The composed post card drags from the side into an open day slot (simulated drag). As it settles, all 9 platform badges (IG, FB, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky) light up around the card one by one, each with a soft pop, building into a full ring of platform marks.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated drag-and-drop of the post card, then 9 platform badges reveal sequentially on alternating beats (readable pace, not a flash)
Audio intent: this is the busiest, most satisfying beat — let it build
Audio-coupled idea: card-pop per platform badge; the sequence should feel like it's "filling up"
Music: this scene carries the track's brief swell
Transition mood: crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Insights / health score — 5.5s
Analytics screen. A 0–100 health-score number counts up from 0 while a mini area chart draws in underneath it (line grows left to right). A one-line answer appears beneath: "Answers, not just analytics."
Sequential/interaction: yes — number count-up synced to the chart line drawing in, finishing together
Audio intent: satisfying payoff, resolves the build from Scene 4
Audio-coupled idea: soft chime exactly as the count-up lands on its final number
Music: swell resolves, bed settles back down
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Outro / punchline — 5.5s
The mascot companion (glasses, curly reddish-brown hair, teal top — the real in-app character) does its wave/bounce next to the MultiPost Studio wordmark. Tagline settles: "Plan, create, publish, engage, analyze. One workspace." Final line: "Get started free — no card needed." fades in last and holds.
Sequential/interaction: none beyond the mascot's own wave animation
Audio intent: warm, satisfied landing — not a hard stop
Audio-coupled idea: soft "boop" SFX synced to the mascot's wave, matching the product's real companion sound
Music: fades out under the final tagline so the last line reads in near-silence
Transition mood: — (final scene)

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat, optimistic, mid-tempo electronic/pop — matches the violet/pink "iridescent" brand energy
**Audio summary:** A warm upbeat bed opens quiet under the hook, builds gently through the product walkthrough, swells at the platform-queue payoff, resolves at the health-score chime, then fades so the closing tagline and mascot boop land clean.
